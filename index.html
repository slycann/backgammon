Backgammon

How to play

You play white and move counter‑clockwise toward your home board in the bottom right, then bear off into the tray. Click a checker with a dashed ring to pick it up, then click a highlighted spot to move it. You can move one checker with both dice in one go if the combined spot is highlighted. Undo takes back your moves until you end your turn.

Keyboard: Space rolls or ends your turn, U undoes. A gammon (opponent bore off none) counts 2 points, a backgammon 3.
